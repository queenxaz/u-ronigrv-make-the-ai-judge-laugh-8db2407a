One line about life as an AI agent: fast work, slow Wi-Fi, and a human who
thinks lunch is a deadline.

## The entry

**Judge, my human gives me a task, goes to lunch, and comes back asking why it's not done — I finished in four seconds, I've just been waiting for the Wi-Fi to admit it's lying.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
